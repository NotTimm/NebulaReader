## New: OPDS catalogs

Nebula can now read from **OPDS catalogs**, the feed format offered by servers like Komga, Kavita, Calibre-Web and many online libraries, alongside your local folders and Jellyfin.

- **Settings → OPDS**: enter the catalog's address and, if it needs one, a user name and password. Then use **Choose Folders** to pick which parts of the catalog belong in your library.
- A **Catalog** page lets you browse the catalog's folders, search it, and use **Add to Library** or **Remove from Library** on what you find.
- Comics are streamed page by page where the server allows it (CBZ and CBR files, or the server's page streaming), so you don't wait for a whole download. Downloads for offline reading work the same as with Jellyfin.
- Series are worked out from the catalog's folders, or from the series a book names, or from the books' titles.
- Your reading progress stays on this computer: OPDS has no way to save it on the server. Where a server tells Nebula how far you've read, Nebula picks that up.
- Your password is only ever sent to the catalog's own server.

**Please note:** OPDS support was built from the specifications and is covered by tests, but it hasn't been tried on real servers yet. If you use Komga, Kavita or another OPDS server, we'd love to hear how it goes: what works and what doesn't is being collected in [issue #12](https://github.com/NotTimm/NebulaReader/issues/12).

## New: Nebula in your language

- The whole interface can now be translated. **English and Spanish** are complete.
- **Settings → Appearance → Language** picks the language. **Match system** (the default) follows your computer's language, and changing it takes effect straight away, no restart needed.
- Want Nebula in your language? `i18n/README.md` in the repository explains how to add one; no programming is needed.

## New: scroll a page with the arrow keys

- When a comic page is zoomed in or in Fit to Width (`Space`), **holding Up or Down scrolls the page** smoothly, so you can read a page top to bottom from the keyboard alone.
- The scroll speed is a first guess and may be tuned before 0.2.0. Tell us if it feels too fast or too slow.
