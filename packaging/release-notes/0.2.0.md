Nebula 0.2.0 is the biggest update yet: **OPDS catalogs** (your own Komga or Kavita server, or public collections like Project Gutenberg), **Wikipedia** inside Nebula if you want it, the interface in **your language**, and a much better **book reader**. Updating keeps your library, progress and settings.

## New: OPDS catalogs

Nebula can now read from **OPDS catalogs**, the feed format offered by servers like Komga, Kavita and calibre-web and by many online libraries, alongside your local folders and Jellyfin. In **Settings → OPDS**, enter the catalog's address (and a username and password if it needs one), press **Connect**, and choose how to use it:

- **Add as a library**, for a server of your own. On the **Catalog** page, press **Add to Library** on the folders you want (a library, "All series"…), and they sync into your library like a Jellyfin library. Comics stream page by page where the server allows it (CBZ and CBR files, or the server's own page streaming), downloads and offline reading work as with Jellyfin, and series are worked out from the server's own series, its folders, or the books' titles. Nebula learns from the first book how your server can send pages, so later books open sooner. Adding the whole catalog, or a folder of a thousand books or more, asks first.
- **Add as a catalog**, for big public collections like **Project Gutenberg** (`https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks.opds/`). Browse and search it, open a book to see its **cover, author, description and editions**, and press **Download**. It joins your library under "Downloads · Project Gutenberg", and **Remove Download** takes it out again. Nothing else is copied to your computer.
  - Each catalog gets a **shelf in the library** (under **OPDS** in the source menu) with its search box and main sections (Gutenberg's Popular, Latest and Random).
  - Downloaded books keep the catalog's description, and classic titles read cleanly ("Moby Dick; Or, The Whale" shows as "Moby Dick").
  - Sort and filter options a catalog offers show as buttons above the list. Tip for Gutenberg: its search takes a language, so `dracula l.en` finds English editions only.

Good to know: OPDS has no way to save reading progress on the server, so progress stays on this computer. Your password is only ever sent to the catalog's own server. OPDS has been tried with Project Gutenberg and Komga; if you use Kavita, calibre-web or another server, please tell us how it goes in [issue #12](https://github.com/NotTimm/NebulaReader/issues/12).

## New: Wikipedia, if you want it

Turn it on in **Settings → Wikipedia → Read Wikipedia in Nebula**, and Wikipedia joins the library's source menu (top left) with its own globe icon. It's drawn by Nebula itself, not a web page, and laid out like the site.

- **The main page as the site shows it today:** From today's featured article, Did you know…, In the news (with Ongoing and Recent deaths), On this day (with births and deaths and links to the days around it), From today's featured list, and Today's featured picture, each with its pictures and every link, plus the most-read articles of the day.
- **Articles** read as one scrolling page with the serif title, the infobox at the right in its own colours, pictures with their captions beside the text, and **tables whose rows and columns line up** like the site's. Blue links open other articles in Nebula; web links open your browser.
- **Contents** in a sidebar, closed until you open it with its button or **C**. Subsections are indented under their section.
- **30 languages:** a menu on the main page's top bar switches between Wikipedias (Deutsch, Français, Español, 日本語 and more). English is the default and your choice is remembered.
- **Select and copy text** across as many paragraphs, headings, lists and tables as you like. Double-click picks a word, triple-click a paragraph, and dragging to the top or bottom edge scrolls the page. **Ctrl+C** copies (**Cmd+C** on a Mac), **Ctrl+A** selects the page, and right-click has **Copy** and **Select All**.
- **Smooth scrolling** like a browser's: the wheel glides and a touchpad swipe coasts. Hold **Up** or **Down** to scroll with the keyboard.
- **Your place is kept** when you open the contents, look at a picture, follow a link and come Back, or pop out to your library and return.
- **Pictures:** click one to see it full window and zoom; **Left** and **Right** step through the page's pictures and **Esc** closes. Pictures load in the background and are kept on disk (up to 400 MB).
- Every article ends with its credit, licence (CC BY-SA 4.0) and **View on Wikipedia**. Nebula isn't made by or connected to Wikipedia or the Wikimedia Foundation, and uses its own icon rather than Wikipedia's logo.

## New: Nebula in your language

- The whole interface can be translated. **English and Spanish** are complete.
- **Settings → Appearance → Language** picks the language. **Match system** (the default) follows your computer's, and changing it takes effect straight away.
- Want Nebula in your language? [i18n/README.md](https://github.com/NotTimm/NebulaReader/blob/master/i18n/README.md) explains how to add one; no programming needed.

## Better book reading

- **Books that put several chapters in one file are split into their real chapters.** Project Gutenberg's EPUBs do this: Moby Dick used to open as 13 huge "chapters" and now has its 143, each with its title in the contents.
- **The top bar says which chapter you're in**, counting only real chapters: Dracula's first chapter is "Chapter 1 of 27", not "Chapter 8" after the cover and title pages, which show their own names (Contents, Preface).
- **Links in books work.** The book's own contents page and links like "see chapter 5" open that chapter. Links are underlined. **Web links ask first** before opening your browser, with **Always open links from this book** to stop asking.
- **A touchpad swipe turns one page**, instead of flipping through several.
- "Opening book…" shows while a book loads, and reading-time estimates are a little more accurate.

## Comics

- **Hold Up or Down to scroll a zoomed page**, or one in Fit to Width (`Space`), so you can read a page top to bottom from the keyboard alone.
- **Touchpad pinch zooms on Windows.** Windows reported a pinch as a plain scroll, so pages panned or nothing happened; and two-finger scrolling on a Windows touchpad is now always taken as a touchpad, not a mouse wheel.

## Beta versions (Windows and Mac)

**Settings → About → Beta versions** lets the Windows installer, the Windows zip and the Mac app follow the betas: Nebula updates itself to each new beta as it comes out. A beta keeps its own copy of your library and settings, so your regular library is never touched, and turning the setting off while on a beta offers to **switch back** to the regular version. Linux packages aren't affected.

**On a beta now?** Nebula updates you to 0.2.0 and keeps beta versions on, with the library you've been using in the betas, so the next beta (0.2.1-beta.1 and so on) comes to you too. Turn **Beta versions** off to stay on regular releases.

## More colour themes

**Settings → Appearance → Colors** has twelve new themes: **Coffee** and **Coffee Cream** (a dark and a light warm brown), Catppuccin Frappé and Macchiato, Everforest Dark, Gruvbox Light, Kanagawa, One Dark, Rosé Pine and Rosé Pine Dawn, Solarized Dark and Tokyo Night.

## Downloads

- The **Downloads** page follows the library's source: everything, or only Jellyfin's or only OPDS's downloads, and says where each series is from.
- The downloads folder in Nebula's data folder is now called `downloads` (it was `jellyfin-downloads`). It's renamed the first time 0.2.0 starts. Downloads in a folder you picked yourself aren't affected.

## Under the hood

- Updated libraries throughout, including networking (reqwest 0.13), the database (rusqlite 0.41) and archive support.
- The README and the contributor guide cover OPDS, Wikipedia and translations.

## Installing

Windows installer, Windows zip and Mac app: Nebula offers this update itself (**Update now**). Fedora: `sudo dnf upgrade nebula-reader` from the COPR once it has built. Other systems: download the package for your system below.
