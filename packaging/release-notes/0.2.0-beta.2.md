> **This is a beta.** It has what's coming in 0.2.0 so you can try it early. Nebula won't offer it to you as an update: install it yourself from this page. From this beta on, Windows and Mac installs can follow the betas by themselves (see below). Going back to 0.1.16 is safe; your library and settings are kept.

## New: add an OPDS catalog as a library or as a catalog

When you connect to an OPDS catalog in **Settings → OPDS**, Nebula now asks how you want to use it:

- **Add as a library**, for a server of your own (Komga, Kavita, calibre-web…). The folders you pick sync into your library and comics stream from the server, as in beta 1. Adding the whole catalog, or a folder of a thousand books or more, now asks first, so a big server can't fill your library by accident.
- **Add as a catalog**, for big public collections like **Project Gutenberg** (`https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks.opds/`). You browse and search it, and only what you download is kept. Nothing else is copied to your computer.

Browsing a catalog:

- Folders and books are listed with their covers. Open a book to see its **cover, author, description and editions** (for example "EPUB3 with images" and "EPUB without images", with their sizes), plus links to more by the same author and on the same subjects.
- **Download** puts the book in your library, in its own group ("Downloads · Project Gutenberg"). **Read** opens it, and **Remove Download** takes it out of the library again.
- **Sort and filter options** a catalog offers ("Newest", "Title", languages…) show as buttons above the list.
- **Esc** goes back a step, a book's page always opens at the top, and **Back** returns to where you were in the list.
- A new search replaces the last one instead of piling up.
- Links in a catalog that lead to a web page (Gutenberg's Facebook and Mastodon pages) offer **Open in Browser**.
- The search button in the library's header, **Browse catalogs**, takes you back to your catalogs. With more than one, you can switch between them at the top of the page.
- Tip for Gutenberg: its search takes a language. `dracula l.en` finds English editions only, and `l.fr` lists French books.

## Better book reading

- **Books that put several chapters in one file are split into real chapters.** Project Gutenberg's EPUBs do this: Moby Dick used to open as 13 huge "chapters", and now has its 143 real ones, each with its own title in the contents.
- **Links in a book work.** A book's own contents page, "see chapter 5" and similar links open that chapter at its first page, in one- and two-page layouts. Links are underlined.
- **Web links ask first.** A link to a website shows the address and asks before opening your browser, with **Always open links from this book** to stop asking for that book.
- **A touchpad swipe turns one page.** Before, one swipe could flip through several pages, which felt like scrolling. A mouse wheel still turns a page per notch.
- The loading screen says "Opening book…" for books instead of "Loading comic…".
- Reading-time estimates are a little more accurate (punctuation after a link or italics no longer counts as a word).

## New: beta versions (Windows and Mac)

**Settings → About → Beta versions** lets the Windows installer, the Windows zip and the Mac app follow the betas:

- **On:** Nebula updates itself to each new beta as it comes out.
- **Off while running a beta:** Nebula offers to **switch back** to the regular version and installs it over the beta.
- A beta keeps **its own copy of your library and settings**, made from your regular ones the first time it starts (downloaded files aren't copied). Your regular library is never touched by a beta, and switching back finds it as you left it.
- A beta installed by hand, like this one, starts with beta versions turned on.

This is the first beta with this setting. If you're on beta 1, install this one by hand once; after that it updates itself. Linux packages aren't affected.

## More colour themes

**Settings → Appearance → Colors** has twelve new themes: **Coffee** and **Coffee Cream** (a dark and a light warm brown), Catppuccin Frappé and Macchiato, Everforest Dark, Gruvbox Light, Kanagawa, One Dark, Rosé Pine and Rosé Pine Dawn, Solarized Dark and Tokyo Night.

## Downloads

- The **Downloads** page follows the library's source: everything, or only Jellyfin's or only OPDS's downloads. Under "All", each series says where it's from. With downloads from both, buttons at the top switch between them.
- The downloads folder in Nebula's data folder is now called `downloads` (it was `jellyfin-downloads`). It's renamed the first time this version starts; downloads in a folder you picked yourself aren't affected.

## Fixed

- Connecting to Project Gutenberg's catalog failed with "the catalog refused the username or password". Nebula dropped the `/` at the end of the address, and Gutenberg refuses the address without it. Both forms are tried now, and a refusal without a username no longer blames one.
- The choice between adding a catalog as a library or a catalog showed as stretched pills; they're rounded cards now.
