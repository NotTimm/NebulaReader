## Wikipedia, polished

Wikipedia (still off unless you turn it on: **Settings → Wikipedia → Read Wikipedia in Nebula**) gets everything you'd reach for while reading:

- **Other languages.** A language menu on the main page's top bar switches between 30 Wikipedias (Deutsch, Français, Español, 日本語, Русский, 中文, Português and more), each listed in its own language. English is the default, and your choice is remembered. Some smaller Wikipedias have no daily main-page feed; search and Random article work in all of them.
- **Select and copy text.** Drag across an article to select it, across as many paragraphs, headings, lists, tables and captions as you like. Double-click picks a word, and triple-click a whole paragraph. Hold the mouse near the top or bottom of the page while selecting and it scrolls, faster the further out you go, so long passages can be copied in one go. **Ctrl+C** copies (**Cmd+C** on a Mac), **Ctrl+A** selects the whole page, and **right-click** offers **Copy** and **Select All**. On Linux the selection also goes to middle-click paste.
- **Smooth scrolling.** The mouse wheel glides to where it's going instead of jumping, and a touchpad swipe keeps coasting after your fingers lift, like a web browser.
- **Arrow keys scroll.** Hold **Up** or **Down** to scroll the page, easing up to speed and gliding to a stop, like the comic reader.
- **C shows or hides the contents.** Subsections in the contents are now indented under their section.
- **Your place is kept.** Showing or hiding the contents, opening a picture, following a link and coming **Back**, or popping out to your library and returning all bring you back to where you were reading.
- **Text no longer runs under a table** that starts partway down a paragraph beside the infobox.

## Windows: touchpad pinch zooms

Pinching on a Windows precision touchpad now zooms comic pages and Wikipedia pictures. Before, Windows reported the pinch as a plain scroll, so the page panned or nothing happened.
