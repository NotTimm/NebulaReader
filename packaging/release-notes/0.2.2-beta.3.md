Everything from [0.2.2-beta.2](https://github.com/NotTimm/NebulaReader/releases/tag/v0.2.2-beta.2) (the Fit to Width fixes) and [0.2.2-beta.1](https://github.com/NotTimm/NebulaReader/releases/tag/v0.2.2-beta.1) (Fandom wikis, the Library menu settings, Direct3D 12 on Windows) is here too, plus:

## Fandom main pages that look like the site

A wiki's main page on Fandom is built out of boxes: Welcome, Featured Article, Did You Know?, and so on. Nebula used to pour all of it into one long column of nested tables. Now:

- **Every box is its own section** with a proper heading, so it's in the contents too.
- **Rows of pictures come through as rows.** A grid of characters shows each portrait with its name and opens that character's page; boxes of affiliated wikis or games become a tidy grid of their logos.
- **The clutter is gone:** chat widgets that only work on the website, ad spaces, and the long lists of other wikis Fandom adds to the bottom of every page.
- A box that's just a picture (a Featured Image) keeps it under its own heading.

Articles keep their infoboxes, picture tabs and pictures as before.

## Your wikis, with their own look

Each wiki you follow now has a card with a **banner**: the wiki's own background art, filling the card, with its logo on top (or the wiki's colour when it has no background art). Logos are found the way Fandom's own page header finds them, so wikis that showed no logo before, like the Attack on Titan Wiki, have one now. Wikis you already follow are brought up to date on their own; there's nothing to re-add.

This was checked against the real Bleach and Attack on Titan wikis. Other wikis build their main pages in their own ways; if one still looks wrong, please [report it](https://github.com/NotTimm/NebulaReader/issues/new/choose) with the wiki's name.

## Also

- Under the hood: libcosmic, the toolkit Nebula is built on, is up to date with its latest changes.
