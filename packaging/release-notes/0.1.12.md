## New: Fit to Width

Press **Space** while reading a comic, or click the new **Fit to Width** button in the header bar, and the page quickly snaps out to the width of the window, starting at its top. Scroll down with the mouse wheel or trackpad to read the rest.

- It's made for reading in a normal-sized window, where a whole page is small.
- While it's on, scrolling moves the page instead of zooming it.
- It stays on as you turn pages, and each new page starts at its top.
- **Space** again (or **Esc**, or **M**) goes back to the whole page. A short note at the bottom of the window says which one you're in.
- It's for comics only. In books, Space still turns the page.
- It isn't remembered: each comic opens showing the whole page.

## Faster and lighter

- **Jellyfin comics open faster.** Nebula used to fetch the table of contents of the chapter before, the current one and the next three one after another before showing anything. Now it fetches them all at once. On a test server with internet-like delay, that went from about half a second to 0.15 s, and the gain is bigger on slower connections.
- **Page turns are quicker.** Pages near the one you're reading are uploaded to the graphics card while you read, so turning to them shows them straight away.
- **Each page takes half the memory.** Decoded pages used to be held twice. With large scans that could reach well over a gigabyte, enough to slow down 8 and 16 GB machines such as many Macs.
- **No more wrong pages.** Opening another comic while pages were still loading could show a page from the previous one. Pages you have already flipped far past are no longer loaded either, so the page in front of you never waits behind them.
- **Syncs and scans no longer freeze the window.** Saving a Jellyfin sync or a folder scan wrote to disk once per row. Each one is now a single save.
- **Less work while reading:**
  - In full screen, moving the mouse no longer redraws the window hundreds of times a second.
  - Opening a large CBR no longer freezes the window while its details are read.
  - Long chapter lists open faster.
  - A first Jellyfin sync no longer refreshes the whole library for every cover it saves.

## Fixes

- **Dark mode no longer turns light by itself.** Nebula checks the system's dark mode now and then. If a check failed (on a Mac this could happen just as a comic opened), it was taken to mean light mode and the whole window turned white. A failed check now leaves the colours alone, on Mac, Windows, GNOME and KDE.
- **Right-click menus are clearer:**
  - A Continue Reading card downloads or removes **just the chapter it shows** (Download Issue / Remove Download).
  - One-shots and books say **Download Book**, on their card and on their page.
  - Whole series keep **Download All** / **Remove Downloads**, also on the Downloads page.
- **Mac:**
  - The hidden `._` files a Mac writes on USB drives and network shares, and the `__MACOSX` folders inside zips made with Finder, no longer show up as broken chapters or pages.
  - Shortcuts use **Cmd** (Cmd 0, Cmd + and Cmd − in books), and letter shortcuts ignore Cmd, so Cmd+C no longer opens the chapter list.
  - Converting a CBR to CBZ moves the old file to the Trash without asking to control Finder first.
  - Quitting with Cmd+Q no longer loses the last few seconds of Jellyfin reading progress.
  - At most 8 comic files stay open at once. Reading a long series could reach the Mac's limit on open files.
- **Windows:**
  - Removing a download works even while that comic is open.
  - A library folder at the root of a drive no longer lists the Recycle Bin or other system folders.

## Updating

Windows and Mac: Nebula offers the update itself (from 0.1.7 on). Fedora (COPR): `sudo dnf upgrade nebula-reader`. Everything else: download the package for your system below.
