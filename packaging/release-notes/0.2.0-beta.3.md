## New: Wikipedia (if you want it)

Nebula can now show Wikipedia, drawn by the app itself rather than a web page, and laid out like the site. It's **off unless you turn it on**: **Settings → Wikipedia → Read Wikipedia in Nebula**.

- **Where it is:** Wikipedia joins the library's source menu at the top left, next to All, Local, Jellyfin and OPDS, with its own globe icon. Pick it to open Wikipedia; the same menu takes you back to your library.
- **The main page:** today's featured article, In the news, On this day, the most-read articles and the picture of the day, in the site's coloured boxes.
- **Search and Random article,** with Back (or Esc) and Main page at the top.
- **Articles read like the site:** one scrolling page with the serif title and "From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia".
  - **Contents** in a sidebar that stays put as you scroll, with each section opening onto its subsections, and **hide** to put it away.
  - **The infobox at the right** with its own colours (the blue "Belligerents" bands, Date and Location rows), and pictures with their captions, with the text running beside them and then across the page, as on Wikipedia.
  - **Blue links** open other articles in Nebula; web links open your browser.
  - **Tables** fill the width, and bar charts draw their bars.
- **Pictures:** click any one to see it full window, zoomable. **Left** and **Right** step through the page's other pictures, and **Esc** closes it. Pictures load in the background at the size they're shown, so pages stay smooth, and are kept on disk (up to 400 MB) so they're quick the next time.
- **Credit:** every article ends with its credit, its licence (CC BY-SA 4.0) and **View on Wikipedia**. Nebula isn't made by or connected to Wikipedia or the Wikimedia Foundation, and uses its own icon rather than Wikipedia's logo.
- English Wikipedia only for now.

## New: catalog shelves in the library

OPDS catalogs you've added as a catalog (like Project Gutenberg) get a **shelf in the library**, shown under **OPDS** in the source menu, or whenever catalogs are all you have:

- Each catalog has a card with **its search box**, its **main sections** (Gutenberg's Popular, Latest and Random) and **Browse**.
- A section or a search opened there starts in the catalog with the right trail ("Project Gutenberg › Popular"); Back and Esc go up to the catalog's start.

## Better catalog downloads

- **Books downloaded from a catalog keep the catalog's description,** shown on the book's page and marked "From the OPDS catalog". A ComicVine or AniList match still takes over if it finds one.
- **Classic titles read cleanly:** "Moby Dick; Or, The Whale" shows as "Moby Dick" in the library; searches still match the full title.
