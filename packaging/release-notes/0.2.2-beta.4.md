## Windows 11: Mica and Acrylic

On Windows 11, Nebula's window can now have the same see-through backgrounds as Windows' own apps, the way it gets frosted glass on COSMIC.

- **Settings → Appearance → Window background** has three choices:
  - **Mica** (the default): your wallpaper, heavily blurred and tinted, showing faintly through the window, as in Windows Settings and File Explorer. It's subtle on purpose.
  - **Acrylic**: frosted glass over whatever is behind the window, other windows included. The more see-through of the two.
  - **Solid**: the plain background, as before.
- A change applies the **next time Nebula starts**.
- It needs **Windows 11 22H2 or later** with **Transparency effects** on (Windows Settings → Personalization → Colors). With that switch off, or with battery saver on, Nebula stays solid.
- The backdrop shows wherever Nebula has no background of its own: the library, Wikipedia and Fandom, and around the page in the comic reader. Theater mode stays black. Books get the frosted pages switch they have on COSMIC.
- Nothing changes on Linux or a Mac.

**This is the first try on real Windows machines.** If the window looks wrong (black, flickering, glitchy when resizing) or feels slower, pick **Solid**. If you can't reach Settings, start Nebula once from PowerShell with a plain window:

```powershell
$env:WGPU_DX12_PRESENTATION_SYSTEM = "Hwnd"; & "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\Nebula Reader\nebula-reader.exe"
```

Please [tell us](https://github.com/NotTimm/NebulaReader/issues/new/choose) how it looks on your PC.
