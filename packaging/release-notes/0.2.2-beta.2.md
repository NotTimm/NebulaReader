Everything from [0.2.2-beta.1](https://github.com/NotTimm/NebulaReader/releases/tag/v0.2.2-beta.1) (Fandom wikis, the Library menu settings, Direct3D 12 on Windows) is here too, plus:

## Fit to Width stays where you are

- **No more jumping back to the top.** If you pressed **Space** for Fit to Width and started scrolling right away (with the arrow keys, the wheel or the touchpad), the page often snapped back to its top once, about a second later. The "Fit to width" note leaving the screen was rebuilding the page viewer, which forgot how far you had scrolled. It no longer does, and neither do opening or closing the chapter list or a banner appearing.
- **Space, Space keeps your place.** Scroll halfway down a page in Fit to Width, press **Space** for a look at the whole page, then **Space** again: you're back at the same spot instead of the top. Turning the page still starts the next one at its top.

## Also

- Under the hood: libcosmic, the toolkit Nebula is built on, is up to date with its latest changes, including a fix for crashes with very large windows.

## Installing the beta

Windows and Mac with beta versions on: Nebula offers it. Otherwise download the package for your system below and install it over your current copy. Fedora COPR and winget stay on 0.2.1; use the `.rpm` or another package below to try the beta on Linux.
