> **This is a beta.** It has what's coming in 0.2.2 so you can try it early. If you're on Windows or a Mac with **Settings → About → Beta versions** on, Nebula offers this update itself; otherwise install it from this page. Going back to 0.2.1 is safe; your library and settings are kept.

## Fandom wikis you follow

Nebula can now read **Fandom wikis**, the same way it reads Wikipedia: drawn by Nebula, not a web page.

- **Turn it on** in **Settings → Library menu → Fandom**. A Fandom page joins the menu at the top left, under Wikipedia.
- **Follow the wikis you like.** It opens on **Your wikis**: type a wiki's name (`Resident Evil`), the part before `.fandom.com` (`residentevil`) or its address (`attackontitan.fandom.com/es` for another language) and press **Follow**. Each wiki gets a card with its logo and colour; click one to open it.
- **Read it like the site.** Each wiki's main page, **search**, **Random article**, and articles on one scrolling page with the contents beside them. Character infoboxes come through with their picture tabs, along with tabbed sections (Anime / Manga…), galleries and the rows of picture links on main pages, all headed in the wiki's own colour.
- **Links to other wikis** open in Nebula too, with a **Follow** button if you don't follow that wiki yet.
- Everything you know from Wikipedia works here: selecting and copying text, smooth and arrow-key scrolling, **C** for the contents, the picture viewer, and **Back**. Wikipedia and Fandom each keep their own place.
- Pages are fetched only when you open them, and every page credits its wiki under its licence, with a **View on Fandom** button. Other MediaWiki sites (wiki.gg, for example) can be followed by their address too.

This is the first go at Fandom: it has been built against Fandom's page layout but not yet tried on many real wikis. If a page looks wrong, please [report it](https://github.com/NotTimm/NebulaReader/issues/new/choose) with the wiki and page name.

## Your menu, your order

**Settings → Library menu** lists every section of the menu at the top left: Local, Jellyfin, OPDS, Wikipedia and Fandom. Move them up and down to put them in the order you like, and switch off the ones you don't use. Wikipedia and Fandom are turned on here now (the old Settings → Wikipedia section is gone; if you had Wikipedia on, it stays on). Local, Jellyfin and OPDS still only show once you have books from them, and switching one off only takes it out of the menu: **All** still shows your whole library.

## Windows: no more black flicker

On some Windows PCs the window flashed black now and then, especially while scrolling a Wikipedia article, and the PC could feel sluggish with Nebula open. Nebula drew through Vulkan, which some graphics drivers (Intel's among them) hand over to Windows badly. It now draws with Direct3D 12, Windows' own graphics system, and prefers the power-saving GPU on laptops with two. Scrolling is smooth again.

## Also

- Long pages no longer rebuild themselves on every mouse-wheel step on some systems.
- Under the hood: libcosmic, the toolkit Nebula is built on, is up to date with its latest changes.
