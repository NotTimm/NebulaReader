Fandom wikis, a menu you can arrange, Fit to Width that stays where you are, and a smoother, see-through window on Windows. This is everything from the four 0.2.2 betas, now as a regular release: Fedora COPR and winget get it too.

## Fandom wikis you follow

Nebula can now read **Fandom wikis**, the same way it reads Wikipedia: drawn by Nebula, not a web page.

- **Turn it on** in **Settings → Library menu → Fandom**. A Fandom page joins the menu at the top left.
- **Follow the wikis you like.** It opens on **Your wikis**: type a wiki's name (`Resident Evil`), the part before `.fandom.com` (`residentevil`) or its address (`attackontitan.fandom.com/es` for another language) and press **Follow**.
- **Each wiki gets a card** with a banner: the wiki's own background art with its logo on top (or the wiki's colour when it has no art).
- **Main pages read like the site.** Every box (Welcome, Featured Article, Did You Know?…) is its own section with a heading, rows of characters show each portrait with its name and open their page, and the clutter is gone: chat widgets, ad spaces and the long lists of other wikis Fandom adds to the bottom of each page.
- **Articles** come through on one scrolling page with the contents beside them: character infoboxes with their picture tabs, tabbed sections (Anime / Manga…), galleries, all headed in the wiki's own colour. **Search** and **Random article** are there too.
- **Links to other wikis** open in Nebula, with a **Follow** button if you don't follow that wiki yet.
- Everything you know from Wikipedia works here: selecting and copying text, smooth and arrow-key scrolling, **C** for the contents, the picture viewer and **Back**. Wikipedia and Fandom each keep their own place.
- Pages are fetched only when you open them, and every page credits its wiki under its licence, with a **View on Fandom** button. Other MediaWiki sites (wiki.gg, for example) can be followed by their address too.

Fandom has been checked against the real Bleach and Attack on Titan wikis. Other wikis build their pages in their own ways; if one looks wrong, please [report it](https://github.com/NotTimm/NebulaReader/issues/new/choose) with the wiki and page name.

## Your menu, your order

**Settings → Library menu** lists every section of the menu at the top left: Local, Jellyfin, OPDS, Wikipedia and Fandom. Move them up and down into the order you like and switch off the ones you don't use. Wikipedia is turned on here now (the old Settings → Wikipedia section is gone; if you had it on, it stays on). Local, Jellyfin and OPDS still only show once you have books from them, and switching one off only takes it out of the menu: **All** still shows your whole library.

## Fit to Width stays where you are

- **No more jumping back to the top.** If you pressed **Space** for Fit to Width and started scrolling right away (arrow keys, wheel or touchpad), the page often snapped back to its top about a second later. It no longer does, and neither does opening or closing the chapter list or a banner appearing.
- **Space, Space keeps your place.** Scroll halfway down a page in Fit to Width, press **Space** for a look at the whole page, then **Space** again: you're back at the same spot instead of the top. Turning the page still starts the next one at its top.

## Windows

- **No more black flicker.** On some PCs (Intel graphics among them) the window flashed black now and then, especially while scrolling Wikipedia, and the PC could feel sluggish with Nebula open. Nebula now draws with Direct3D 12, Windows' own graphics system, and prefers the power-saving GPU on laptops with two.
- **Mica and Acrylic on Windows 11.** **Settings → Appearance → Window background** offers **Mica** (the default: your wallpaper, blurred and tinted, showing faintly through, as in Windows Settings), **Acrylic** (frosted glass over whatever is behind the window) or **Solid** (as before). A change applies the next time Nebula starts. It needs Windows 11 22H2 or later with **Transparency effects** on; theater mode stays black. If the window ever looks wrong, pick **Solid**, or start Nebula once from PowerShell with a plain window:

  ```powershell
  $env:WGPU_DX12_PRESENTATION_SYSTEM = "Hwnd"; & "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\Nebula Reader\nebula-reader.exe"
  ```

Nothing changes in how Nebula looks on Linux or a Mac.

## Also

- Long pages no longer rebuild themselves on every mouse-wheel step on some systems.
- Under the hood: libcosmic, the toolkit Nebula is built on, is up to date with its latest changes, including a fix for crashes with very large windows and Wayland theme-update fixes; dependency updates.

## Installing

Windows and Mac: Nebula offers the update itself. Fedora: `sudo dnf upgrade nebula-reader` once the COPR build is done (or `sudo dnf copr enable timbo-slice/nebula-reader` and `sudo dnf install nebula-reader` the first time). Everything else: download the package for your system below.

If you were on a 0.2.2 beta with **Beta versions** on, you stay on the beta channel; switch it off in **Settings → About** to follow regular releases.
