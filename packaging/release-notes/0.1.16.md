## Fixed: Continue Reading was missing comics you started on Jellyfin

When you signed in to a Jellyfin server, Continue Reading only showed a series if you had finished at least one of its chapters, or opened it in Nebula. Anything you were partway through on the Jellyfin website or in another app, with nothing finished yet, was left out, so the shelf showed fewer comics than Jellyfin's own Continue Reading.

- **Comics and books with a page saved on your server now show up in Continue Reading** after the next sync, and the card opens the chapter you were reading, at your page.
- Nebula still shows **one card per series**, the chapter you read most recently. Jellyfin shows every partly read chapter, so it can list the same series more than once.
- The progress bar on such a card stays empty until you've opened that chapter once in Nebula, because Nebula only learns a chapter's page count when it opens it.

## Under the hood

- Fedora (COPR): the package uses Fedora's own zstd library instead of a bundled copy.
- New contributor guide (`CONTRIBUTING.md`) and issue and pull request templates on GitHub. Bug reports and pull requests are welcome.

## Updating

Windows and Mac: Nebula offers the update itself (from 0.1.7 on). Fedora (COPR): `sudo dnf upgrade nebula-reader`. Everything else: download the package for your system below.
